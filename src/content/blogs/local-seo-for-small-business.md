When someone searches for a service near them, the first thing they see is the map pack — three businesses with photos, ratings and directions. Ranking there beats ranking first in the blue links, because it captures the intentiest traffic on the web.

Local SEO is not a mystery. It is a set of specific, repeatable actions around your profile, your reviews, your citations and your location pages.

## 1. Optimize your Google Business Profile completely

An incomplete profile is a profile that loses to a complete one. Fill out every field Google offers.

- Choose the most specific primary category available, then add relevant secondary ones.
- Write a business description using natural language, not keyword stuffing.
- Add your service areas and hours, including holiday hours.
- Upload real photos of your team, premises and work — refreshed monthly.
- List every service with its own short description.
- Enable messaging and answer questions in the Q&A section yourself.

## 2. Build a steady review engine

Reviews are the strongest local ranking factor you directly control, and they convert. The key is consistency rather than bursts.

1. Ask every satisfied customer at the moment of delivery.
2. Send a direct review link so it takes one tap.
3. Respond to every review — positive and negative — within two days.
4. Never buy reviews; the risk far outweighs the short-term gain.
5. Include service keywords naturally in your replies.

> [!NOTE] Volume and recency both matter
> Twenty reviews from three years ago rank worse than ten reviews from the last six months. Keep the flow steady rather than front-loaded.

## 3. Fix and grow your citations

Citations are mentions of your business name, address and phone number across the web. Consistency matters more than volume.

- Audit your NAP — name, address, phone — for exact consistency everywhere.
- Claim the major directories relevant to your country and industry.
- Remove or correct duplicate and outdated listings.
- Add local chamber of commerce and association listings where available.

## 4. Create genuinely useful location pages

If you serve multiple areas, one page per location can rank well — but only if each page is meaningfully different. Duplicate location pages are a common and damaging mistake.

- Unique content specific to that area: local landmarks, coverage details, testimonials.
- Embedded map and clear directions.
- Local phone number or service-area details.
- Internal links from your main services pages.
- LocalBusiness structured data with the correct area served.

## 5. Add local structured data

```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "LocalBusiness",
  "name": "Your Business",
  "address": {

  "openingHours": "Mo-Fr 09:00-18:00"

```

## 6. Track what is working

Use the data inside your Business Profile and Search Console to see which queries and actions are growing.

- Profile calls, direction requests and website clicks.
- Search terms triggering your map listing.
- Review velocity and average rating over time.
- Local landing page rankings in Search Console.

> Local SEO rewards the business that is most present, most reviewed and most consistent — not necessarily the biggest.

## The takeaway

Complete your profile, build a steady review habit, keep citations consistent, write location pages that are actually unique, and add local structured data. Do that consistently and the map pack becomes your most reliable lead source.

:::faq
Q: How long does local SEO take to work?
A: Profile improvements can move rankings within two to four weeks. Building durable top-three map pack presence typically takes three to six months of consistent effort.

Q: Do reviews really affect local rankings?
A: Yes. Review volume, rating and recency are among the strongest factors you can directly influence, and they heavily influence click-through as well.

Q: Do I need a physical address to rank locally?
A: You need a genuine presence. Service-area businesses can rank without a public address, but you must follow Google's guidelines and verify your service areas properly.
:::
